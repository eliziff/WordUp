The journal marker primitive in WordUpStructure.bas adapts `src/journal.rs::title` from the local legal-structure checkout at revision f73bcd5d66575c0504e97fc2c3b95bf5589377e5 (MIT; LICENSE alongside source). VBA adapter whitespace handling covers spaces, tabs and nonbreaking spaces; it does not claim Rust Unicode-whitespace parity. Marker recognition deliberately does not assign a heading level or validate Roman arithmetic. Word-native outline and containment evidence are collected separately. The local legal-pdf-structure graph code informed the separation between evidence and hierarchy; no PDF extraction code is copied.

`ladder.go` ports the sequence-selection order of legal-pdf-support `pairing_support.rs::parse_heading_ladder` at e1b060bdf9b92b9f6295bfc1e7933bd5a446af22. It retains per-candidate actions but does not yet port family statistics or the upstream paragraph eligibility/formatting gates; callers must not promote grammar output directly to headings. The Word adapter caps hierarchy at Word's nine heading levels. PDF-LICENSE contains the upstream MIT license.

`markers.go` adapts the upstream multiple-interpretation approach and Roman conversion. Its recognizer covers simple Roman, letter and decimal-integer labels, parenthesized markers, and Part/Chapter dash labels; compound decimal numbering and Unicode digits remain outside its current coverage. Sequence observations are explicitly not heading decisions.
